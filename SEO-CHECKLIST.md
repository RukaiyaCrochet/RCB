## ⚠️ URGENT - Do These First!

### 1. COMPRESS IMAGES (CRITICAL!)
Your images are 6-9 MB each. This KILLS page speed!
- Go to: https://tinypng.com or https://squoosh.app
- Upload all product images
- Download compressed versions (target: <500KB each)
- Replace in `/src/assets/` folders

### 2. FIX FILE EXTENSIONS
Rename these files from .PNG to .png:
```
/src/assets/product/handbag/bag4.PNG → bag4.png
/src/assets/product/handbag/bag6_model1.PNG → bag6_model1.png
```
